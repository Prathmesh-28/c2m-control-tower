Meesho’s side · Predicting demand

How Meesho joins what buyers want with what factories make, and tells each factory how much to make

Meesho already sees every search, cart, order and return. Factories give it their catalogue and capacity. Meesho maps both to the same product, predicts demand by district and size, splits it across the right factories, and checks every week whether the numbers it gave were right. It also uses last year’s sales, so festivals and seasons are built in.
