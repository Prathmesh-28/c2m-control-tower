Shared inventory: where every pack is, where it goes next, and when to pull from the factory

The factory and the Factory Node share the storage. The factory keeps the bulk of its stock at its own unit; the node keeps only a few days’ worth near the buyers. Four rules decide how much sits where, and when stock moves.

Target stock

How much should sit at the node?

node orders a day × (days to next truck + 1 day on the road + safety days)
Kavin 67 × (7 + 1 + 2) = 670 packs. Everything above that stays at the factory.

Choosing the node

Where a Factory Node goes: follow the manufacturers

A node only pays off where many registered manufacturers sit close together. So the location is picked in three steps, from the whole country down to one street.

Open a node in a cluster when
  • its cluster score puts it in the next wave
  • at least 40 registered manufacturers that pass the price check sit within 20 km
  • a Valmo lane already serves the area
Put it where
  • the most manufacturers are within 15 km, so trucks are short
  • the average trip from a factory is shortest
  • it is within 5 km of the highway to the Valmo hub
Add a second site when
  • the first runs above 70% full for 4 weeks (the deck’s node KPI)
  • about 3 sites per cluster by Year 4, at ₹1.3 cr each
